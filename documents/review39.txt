I'm nearing a decade out from my time at Northwestern, and my love for the school has only increased since graduation. The school's brand has proved its worth manifold over the course of my career, and the alumni network has proved invaluable. I currently live in NYC, with short stints in Europe, and everywhere I go, I am always so happy to bump into fellow 'Cats.

The campus is just the ideal setting — nestled on the shores of Lake Michigan, right at the doorsteps of the most underrated city in America, Chicago. My classmates were all friendly, smart, inspiring, and driven — and (now that I live in the Northeast), I realize they were also half as insufferable (ha!) as their peers from many other "elite" schools. I'm lucky that my friends from Northwestern have all found success in their respective fields. I'm still incredibly close with many of them.

I still miss those impossibly beautiful days lounging on the Lakefill. Can't recommend NU enough!
