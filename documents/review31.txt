Attending a prestigious and competitive university brings both advantages and challenges. One of the most difficult adjustments has been adapting to the quarter system after coming from a high school with semesters. The transition was harder than I anticipated, and although I’ve adapted to some extent, I still find the pace challenging. Academically, the classes are far more demanding than I expected, especially coming from a background where I was one of the top students. On the other hand, the student and social life here has been a positive and enjoyable aspect. I’ve found a close-knit group of friends who share similar interests, like watching movies on Fridays, and I love joining other Northwestern students for daily pickup soccer games. The sports events I’ve attended have been engaging, and the social scene is lively, especially for a smaller school. Overall, the balance between academics and social life here has made for a well-rounded and rewarding experience.
