The 90s were a different time. While NU is a larger, private school, I really had a small school, community feel. Lived in the Residential College system (CRC & Willard) was great for getting to know people, appreciate traditions, and feel like you're at home. The campus is beautiful, along Lake Michigan. It's easily walkable from one side to the other, even in bad weather. Bikes are still common to see. I loved my time there and had hoped one of my sons would become Wildcats as well. On a recent visit, I noted that there wasn't much updated at Norris, the student life building. New buildings on campus that I toured were beautiful, with a lot to offer undergraduates. I can speak to the advantages of saying that I am a Northwestern alum. It has opened many doors for me, even in central Pennsylvania. The alumni network is incredible.
