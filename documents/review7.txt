STAYING ON-CAMPUS THROUGH THE WINTER BREAK: You will need continuous housing and you will be required to live in the South area (because all the continuous housing dorms are located there). This actually puts limits to your dorm options (you will choose from only 3 dorms basically). But other than this little issue, Northwestern is an amazing school with great opportunities. As an international student, I would say their support system for us is very good. Also, it's the only school that accepted me with full-ride after getting rejected by 50 colleges. It lived up to my hypes
