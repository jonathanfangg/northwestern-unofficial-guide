Northwestern has overall been a very enjoyable university to attend. The Professors who lead classes are incredibly knowledgeable about the subjects they teach, and they strive to make all the classes interesting. While everything is very difficult, professors take the time to thoroughly explain everything and are there to help the students. Outside the classroom, Evanston is a nice town. Downtown is a short walk away and Chicago is a train ride to downtown. The food on campus is not the best, but they are working towards developing new ideas and making it better.
